Orbit

An audio-first travel guide that pings you about places worth a stop.

Orbit runs in the background and gives you a quick, conversational heads-up when something nearby fits what you're into — hands-free, through whatever earbuds you're already wearing.

Join the TestFlight beta View on GitHub

How it works

  • Detects nearby curated points of interest as you walk
  • Voice-pings you only when something matches your taste profile
  • Conversational follow-ups powered by an LLM
  • Hands off to Apple Maps or Google Maps for directions
